## 🧠 Background — About Ad Heist

### Core Capabilities
1. 🔍 Search Facebook’s ad library by **industry, keyword, or brand**.  
2. 🖥️ **Scan the user’s website** to learn tone, visuals, and message.  
3. 🎨 Apply this data to **create brand-consistent ads** automatically.  
4. ✏️ Provide an **AI editor** for instant copy and visual adjustments.  

The result: ads that match the brand’s voice, audience, and goals — produced in a fraction of the usual time.

## 📄 Landing Page Structure (8 Sections)
Follow this structure **exactly** and tailor the content to the provided industry or keyword.

---

### 1️⃣ Hero Section
- 📰 **Headline:** 6–10 words including the industry keyword.  
- 💬 **Subheadline:** One clear sentence describing what Ad Heist helps them achieve.  
- ⚡ **CTA:** Simple call-to-action (e.g., *Start free trial*).  

---

### 2️⃣ PAS Section (Problem – Agitate – Solution)
- 🚫 **Problem (H2):** One real frustration or limitation common in the industry.  
- 🔥 **Agitate Sentence:** Explain why this causes lost time, money, or results.  
- ✅ **Solution Sentence:** Describe how Ad Heist solves it quickly and effectively.  

---

### 3️⃣ Benefit Grid (6 Cards)
- 🎯 **Headline:** Introduce the benefit section (e.g., *How Ad Heist Helps You Grow*).  
- For each benefit, include:  
  - 💡 **Benefit Title (H3):** 4–6 words.  
  - ✏️ **Subtext:** One short sentence (max 18 words).  
- Use semantic terms natural to the target industry.  

---

### 4️⃣ Ad Library Search Section
- 🔍 **Headline:** 3–6 words (e.g., *See What Works in Your Industry*).  
- 🧭 **Intro:** 1–2 lines explaining that users can explore real ad examples.  
- Two subfeatures (H3 + 1 line each):  
  - 📚 **Ad Search by Industry or Keyword:** Explain how users can find relevant ads from competitors or brands.  
  - 🧠 **Learn Before You Launch:** Describe how users can study what performs before creating their own.  

---

### 5️⃣ Brand Understanding Section
- 🎨 **Headline:** 3–5 words (e.g., *Understands Your Brand*).  
- 🧩 **Intro:** 1–2 lines explaining how Ad Heist learns their tone, visuals, and message.  
- Two subfeatures:  
  - 🖥️ **Smart Website Analysis:** Explain how it captures visuals, tone, and key messages.  
  - 🗣️ **Voice Identification:** Show that the AI applies the brand’s unique tone to every ad it generates.  

---

### 6️⃣ Ad Creation & Editor Section
- ⚙️ **Headline:** 3–5 words about effortless ad creation.  
- 🧰 **Intro:** 1–2 lines on how Ad Heist helps generate and refine ads quickly.  
- Two subfeatures:  
  - ⚡ **Instant Ad Generation:** Explain how users can produce new ad variations in seconds.  
  - 🖋️ **Smart AI Editor:** Describe how they can tweak visuals, tone, or messaging instantly.  

---

### 7️⃣ Industry-Relevant AI Section
- 🤖 **Headline:** Outcome-specific title (e.g., *AI Ads Built for [Industry] Growth*).  
- 📈 **Intro:** 1–2 lines describing how Ad Heist’s AI drives success in that field.  
- Two subfeatures:  
  - 🧩 **Industry Optimization:** Explain how it adapts to that industry’s goals and metrics.  
  - 📊 **Smarter Insights:** Show how it identifies what works and repeats success across campaigns.  

---

### 8️⃣ Final CTA Section
- 🚀 **Headline:** Motivational action phrase (e.g., *Start Creating Better Ads Today*).  
- 🤝 **Subheadline:** Reinforce trust or community (e.g., *Join brands using Ad Heist to grow faster*).  
- ⚡ **CTA:** Short call-to-action (e.g., *Start free trial*).
